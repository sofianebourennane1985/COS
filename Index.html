About the Committee and Members

The Social Works Committee of the Houari Boumediene University of Science and Technology works to provide social support and comprehensive care for all employees, professors, and workers, both active and retired, with the aim of fostering a spirit of solidarity and upgrading the work environment within the university institution.

Available Social Services and Grants

The Social Works Committee of the Houari Boumediene University of Science and Technology offers a variety of grants and assistance to support employees, professors, and workers:

💍 Marriage Grant

A financial grant provided to the employee or professor upon marriage, valued at 50,000 DZD (once throughout the professional career).

👶 Birth Grant

Financial assistance valued at 5,000 DZD granted to workers and professors upon welcoming a new baby.

✂️ Circumcision Grant

A dedicated grant valued at 5,000 DZD to help workers and employees with the circumcision expenses of their children.

🎒 Schooling Grant (School Bag)

Financial assistance provided to workers on the occasion of the school start, valued at 5,000 DZD per schooling child.

🖤 Death and Condolence Grant

Financial support provided in cases of death to ease burdens, according to the following details:

  • • Death of employee (handed to family): 120,000 DZD
  • • Death of husband or wife: 60,000 DZD
  • • Death of mother or father: 20,000 DZD
  • • Death of son or daughter: 20,000 DZD
  • • In case of employee death, an additional grant of 10,000 DZD is added for each child under 18 years old.

🌙 Ramadan Grant

Seasonal assistance allocated to university workers on the occasion of the holy month of Ramadan (Value varies according to the financial capability of the Social Works Committee).

🐑 Eid al-Adha Grant

A seasonal financial grant given to workers to contribute to purchasing the Eid sacrifice (Value varies according to the financial capability of the Social Works Committee).

🏥 Medical Coverage (Prise en charge)

Health coverage and support for medical examinations, tests, and surgical operations via concluded agreements.

💰 Social Loan (Salafia)

Providing exceptional interest-free loans repaid in comfortable monthly installments to face emergency circumstances.

🕋 Umrah Trips

Organizing the annual draw to perform Umrah rituals for the benefit of university workers, professors, and retirees.

🏖️ Summer Camps and Trips

Organizing family summer trips and camps in coastal wilayas for the benefit of workers and their children.

🏕️ Spring Holidays and Trips

Organizing short recreational and exploratory trips during the spring holiday and academic break.
